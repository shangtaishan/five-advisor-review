# Contribution Guide

**Languages:** [中文](CONTRIBUTING.md) · [English](CONTRIBUTING.en.md) · [日本語](CONTRIBUTING.ja.md)

Contributions of real cases, counterexamples, evaluation methods, output improvements, and adaptations for other languages or industries are welcome.

## Pre-submission checklist

- Do not describe multi-role output from one model as evidence from independent models.
- Do not add unsourced market data, failure cases, probabilities, or industry thresholds.
- If a new number is unverified, mark it `[Experiment threshold]` and explain why it was set.
- Do not invent a minority view merely for balance; when none exists, report the strongest counterargument check.
- Do not let the chair introduce factual premises absent from the first round and cross-review.
- Keep the sequence: independent advisor views → cross-review → chair decision.
- When changing a public explanatory document, update its Chinese, English, and Japanese counterparts in the same change.

## Recommended contributions

- Evaluation sets for anonymous cross-review or shared-source checks;
- Validation-action templates for different industries;
- Pre-tests, validation processes, and back-tests for real decisions;
- Cases proving that the framework fails and proposals to improve it;
- Localization and accessibility improvements.

## Issue suggestions

When filing an issue or proposal, state the proposal, goal, audience, constraints, decision at hand, and critical unknowns. The discussion itself can then be reviewed by this framework.
